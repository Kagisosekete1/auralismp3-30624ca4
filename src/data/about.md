### WHO WE ARE

Auralis is a modern digital music streaming and artist discovery platform developed to bring music lovers and creators together in one connected environment.

Created under Se-Mo Group Tech, Auralis represents a vision for a more accessible, inclusive, and artist-friendly digital music experience.

Based in Sandton, Johannesburg, South Africa, our platform is designed with an international audience in mind.

We believe great music should have the opportunity to be discovered, whether it comes from an established recording artist, an emerging independent musician, a local producer, or a talented creator releasing their first song.

Auralis is built around the idea that technology should make music easier to discover, enjoy, share, and publish.

### OUR VISION

Our vision is to build a globally recognised digital music platform that connects artists and listeners across countries, cultures, languages, and musical genres.

We want Auralis to become a welcoming home for music discovery and a valuable platform for independent creative expression.

We envision a music ecosystem where talented creators can showcase their work without unnecessary barriers and where listeners can discover extraordinary music beyond traditional industry boundaries.

### OUR MISSION

Our mission is to make music streaming and independent artist publishing accessible through a modern, reliable, and user-friendly digital platform.

We aim to support music discovery, respect creative ownership, promote responsible publishing, and create meaningful connections between musicians and their audiences.

### BUILT FOR MUSIC LOVERS

Music is more than entertainment. It carries stories, memories, emotions, cultures, and experiences.

Auralis is designed for people who enjoy exploring different sounds, discovering new artists, and building personal connections with the music they love.

From Amapiano and Afro House to Hip-Hop, Gospel, R&B, Jazz, Pop, and many other genres, our goal is to celebrate musical diversity.

### BUILT FOR INDEPENDENT ARTISTS

We recognise the dedication required to write, record, produce, and release original music.

Independent artists often need accessible digital tools to showcase their talent and build an audience.

Auralis provides a platform where eligible creators can publish music, present their artistic identity, and make their work available for discovery.

Artists retain ownership of their original creative works.

We believe protecting artistic ownership is an essential part of building a responsible music platform.

### OUR CORE VALUES

**Creativity:** We celebrate originality, innovation, and artistic expression.

**Accessibility:** We believe music technology should be approachable and useful to a broad audience.

**Ownership:** We respect artists' rights to their original recordings and creative works.

**Discovery:** We support opportunities for listeners to explore unfamiliar artists, sounds, and genres.

**Transparency:** We aim to communicate clearly about platform rules, copyright, monetisation, and privacy.

**Community:** We believe music creates connections between people from different backgrounds.

**Security:** We recognise the importance of protecting user accounts, information, and digital content.

**Continuous Improvement:** We are committed to developing and refining the platform as technology and user needs evolve.

### A PLATFORM WITH SOUTH AFRICAN ROOTS

Auralis is proudly developed under a South African technology brand.

South Africa has a rich and internationally influential music culture, from traditional sounds and Gospel to House, Amapiano, Hip-Hop, and contemporary African music.

Our roots inspire us to value musical diversity while building a platform intended to welcome artists and listeners from around the world.

Auralis is not limited to one country, language, or genre.

Our ambition is to create a digital environment where music can connect people across borders.

### OUR APPROACH TO ARTIST OWNERSHIP

Auralis respects the rights of creators.

Artists retain ownership of their original music and artwork, subject to applicable third-party rights.

Our platform requires limited permissions to host and stream uploaded music, but uploading does not automatically transfer copyright ownership.

We encourage creators to protect their work, provide accurate credits, and obtain all necessary permissions before publishing.

### OUR APPROACH TO MONETISATION

Auralis's current free-platform model focuses on music access, publishing, and discovery.

We do not currently promise per-stream royalties or guaranteed artist earnings.

If monetisation features are introduced, their conditions will be explained separately and transparently.

### OUR COMMITMENT TO PRIVACY

We understand that trust is essential to every digital service.

Auralis is committed to handling personal information responsibly, applying appropriate security safeguards, and respecting applicable data protection rights.

Users may request assistance with their personal information or account deletion through our official support contact.

### LOOKING TOWARDS THE FUTURE

Our long-term ambition is to continue developing Auralis into a capable music streaming ecosystem.

Potential future developments may include enhanced discovery tools, improved artist analytics, additional promotional opportunities, and new services for creators.

These represent development ambitions rather than promises that specific features are currently available.

We believe that a great music platform should evolve alongside the artists and listeners who use it.
