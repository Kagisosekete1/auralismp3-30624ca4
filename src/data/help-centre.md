### GENERAL QUESTIONS

**1. What is Auralis?**

Auralis is a digital music streaming and discovery platform designed for listeners, independent artists, producers, and music enthusiasts. It allows users to discover and enjoy music while providing artists with an accessible space to publish and showcase their work.

**2. Is Auralis similar to Spotify?**

Yes. Auralis follows a music streaming model that allows listeners to discover songs, explore artists, and enjoy music through a digital platform. However, Auralis is an independently operated service with its own features, policies, and business model.

**3. Is Auralis free to use?**

Auralis is intended to provide free music listening and artist uploads under its current service model. Optional paid features may be introduced in the future.

**4. Is Auralis available worldwide?**

Auralis is designed for an international audience. Actual availability may depend on app distribution settings, music licensing, legal restrictions, and technical availability in particular countries.

**5. Who owns Auralis?**

Auralis is operated by Se-Mo Group Tech, based in Sandton, Johannesburg, South Africa.

**6. Can anyone listen to music?**

Eligible users may listen to music made available through the platform, subject to applicable terms and any regional restrictions.

### ACCOUNTS AND PROFILES

**7. How do I create an account?**

Open Auralis, select Sign Up, provide the required registration information, and complete the account creation process.

**8. Do I need an account to listen?**

Some features may require registration. Account requirements depend on the functionality offered in the current application version.

**9. Can I change my username?**

Where profile editing is supported, you can update eligible profile information through your account settings.

**10. What should I do if I forget my password?**

Use the Forgot Password feature where available. If you cannot recover your account, contact semogrouptech@gmail.com.

**11. Can I use Auralis on more than one device?**

Multi-device access depends on the supported application version, authentication system, and account security controls.

**12. How do I permanently delete my account?**

Use the Delete Account feature in the application or submit a deletion request to semogrouptech@gmail.com.

### MUSIC LISTENING

**13. Can I listen to unlimited music?**

Auralis is designed to support music streaming without a per-song listening charge under its free service model. Access remains subject to availability, reasonable technical limitations, and applicable Terms.

**14. Can I search for artists and songs?**

Auralis is designed to help listeners discover songs, albums, and artists through its music catalogue.

**15. Can I create playlists?**

Where supported, users can create personal playlists to organise their favourite songs.

**16. Can I save my favourite music?**

Available library and favourites features allow users to organise music they enjoy.

**17. Can I download songs for offline listening?**

Offline listening is available only if explicitly supported by the application. Streaming access does not automatically grant permission to download or redistribute audio files.

**18. Why has a song disappeared?**

A song may become unavailable because the artist removed it, copyright concerns were identified, licensing changed, or technical restrictions apply.

**19. Can I share music with friends?**

You may use supported sharing features to share song or artist links. Sharing does not grant permission to redistribute copyrighted audio files.

### ARTIST UPLOADS

**20. Who can upload music?**

Independent artists and other authorised rights holders may upload music if they satisfy account eligibility requirements and possess the necessary rights.

**21. Is uploading music free?**

Under the current free-upload model, Auralis does not charge a standard fee simply to submit eligible music to the platform.

**22. Do I keep ownership of my music?**

Yes. Artists retain ownership of their original recordings and other creative works, subject to the rights of collaborators and third parties.

**23. Does Auralis own my songs after uploading?**

No. Uploading grants Auralis limited permission to host, stream, display, and promote the music under the Terms of Use. It does not transfer copyright ownership.

**24. Can I upload an album or EP?**

Auralis is intended to support independent music releases. Available release types depend on the current artist upload tools.

**25. Can I upload cover songs?**

Only where you have obtained the permissions and licences required for the relevant recording, composition, and intended use.

**26. Can I upload a beat purchased online?**

Only if the beat licence authorises the intended distribution and streaming use. Some licences impose restrictions or require additional permissions.

**27. Can I upload a remix?**

You must possess the necessary rights or permissions for any protected recordings or compositions used in the remix.

**28. Can I use copyrighted samples?**

Only with appropriate permission or another valid legal basis. Purchasing or downloading a sample does not automatically grant unlimited distribution rights.

**29. Can I upload AI-generated music?**

AI-assisted or AI-generated music may be subject to additional platform requirements. Uploaders must have sufficient rights to distribute the content and must not infringe the rights or identities of others.

**30. Can I remove my songs?**

Yes. Artists may use supported removal controls or contact support to request removal of their content.

### ARTIST PAYMENTS AND MONETISATION

**31. Does Auralis pay artists per stream?**

No per-stream royalty payment is currently promised under the free-platform model.

**32. Will Auralis introduce artist payments?**

Artist monetisation may be considered in the future. Any payment programme would require separate eligibility rules, agreements, and published payment terms.

**33. Does Auralis distribute music to Spotify or Apple Music?**

Not automatically. Auralis uploads are for the Auralis platform unless a separate external distribution service is explicitly offered.

**34. Can artists promote their music?**

Auralis is intended to help artists improve visibility through profiles, discovery, and available sharing or promotional features.

**35. Are artist statistics available?**

Artist analytics may be available depending on the current version and supported features.

### COPYRIGHT AND SAFETY

Yes. Auralis may remove or restrict content following valid rights complaints or other violations of its Terms.
